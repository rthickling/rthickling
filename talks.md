# Talks, webinars and community

I speak and teach about trading systems, Python, C++ and practical algorithmic trading. This is a selection of presentations, live demonstrations and discussions I've contributed to.

## PyData London

- **[Making Python Faster](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-london-meetup/events/316770322/)** — 6 October 2026, 110th Meetup at Birkbeck, University of London. Lightning talk on combining Python with C++ and generating bindings using reflection. The accompanying [Merton Market Maker project](https://github.com/rthickling/merton-market-maker) provides an engineering demonstration using online jump-diffusion calibration and paper quoting.
- **Why Python is the best language for algorithmic trading** — 7 October 2025, [PyData London](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-london-meetup/), 100th Meetup. Practical Python trading workflows, from data and strategy development to a live bot demonstration.

## ProfitView technical presentations

- **[C++ and Python for Algorithmic Trading](https://profitview.net/events/cpp-python-algo-trading)** — 20 December 2022. My session demonstrates retrieving Parquet trading data in C++ and displaying the results through Python. Antony Peacock and Rainer Grimm contribute separate presentations on mixed-language trading systems and Python/C++ integration.
- **[C++ Concepts and Fireside Chat on Algorithmic Trading](https://profitview.net/events/cpp-concepts-low-latency-algo-trading)** — 4 October 2022. Co-host and fireside speaker with Antony Peacock, discussing low-latency engineering and careers in trading technology. Rainer Grimm presents the C++ Concepts session.
- **[C++ 20 Techniques for Algorithmic Trading](https://profitview.net/events/algo-trading-cpp-20-ranges)** — 26 April 2022. Live C++ trading-strategy demonstration with risk and P&L monitoring through ProfitView, alongside Antony Peacock's industry perspective and Rainer Grimm's presentation on Ranges.
